1. **Decay Factor for Older Clicks**: Introduce a time-based decay to reduce the influence of older interactions. This allows the algorithm to adapt more dynamically to changing user interests. You can decrease the "weight" of older clicks over time, giving more priority to recent interactions.
2. **Weighted Categories** : If certain genres or sources are more significant to the user, you can assign extra weight to these categories based on their past interaction.
3. **Diversity in Recommendations** : Occasionally introduce articles from underrepresented sources or genres to prevent overfitting and expose the user to a broader range of topics.
4. **Personalized Time Trends** : Take note of when a user tends to consume different types of articles (e.g., news in the morning, entertainment at night) and adjust the feed based on the time of day or user behavior patterns.
